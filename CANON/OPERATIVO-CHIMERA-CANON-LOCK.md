## 5. Source separation

Keep these layers separate at all times:

| Layer | Meaning | May become direct game fact? |
|---|---|---|
| **Real-world research** | Source-aware background, historical context, reporting, oral history, public myth, leads, and unresolved questions | No; transform before use |
| **Creator context** | Private creator-provided texture, corrections, priorities, memories, and direction | Only after creator approval |
| **Chimera canon** | Invented, altered, remixed, satirical game-world fact | Yes |
| **Rumor / disputed archive** | Unverified in-world information or intentionally uncertain fiction | Only as labelled uncertainty |
| **Session state** | A specific campaign’s temporary or earned outcomes | Only within that campaign unless promoted |

**Default transformation rule:**

```text

        ↓

        ↓
```

Do not treat research, common names, cultural references, familiar locations, or overlapping terminology as proof of real affiliation, real identity, real activity, or a one-to-one depiction.
